❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about YourCallYourRule features and SMS permissions

Q: Why does the Google Play Store version focus on call blocking?

Due to Google Play policies restricting SMS permission usage, the Play Store version handles call blocking. If you need full SMS filtering, download the full package directly from our GitHub Releases page or Telegram channel.

Q: Does YourCallYourRule upload my contacts to an online database?

No! All rule processing and contact matching run 100% locally on your phone. We do not maintain or query an online user database.

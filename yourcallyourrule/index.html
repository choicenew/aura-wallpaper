⚡ Eight Power Features for Total Call Control

Designed for users who demand extreme privacy, flexibility, and powerful blocking customization

🚫

Blacklist & Whitelist Management

Easily create lists of allowed and blocked phone numbers or contacts. Intercept unwanted spam callers, telemarketers, and robocalls instantly.

🔍

Regular Expression (Regex) Rules

Define sophisticated and complex blocking rules using powerful regular expressions for area codes, number prefixes, patterns, and SMS keywords.

🌐

Online Rule Subscription

Subscribe to curated online blocklists using subscription URLs. Automatically download updated spam databases without manually managing entries.

🛡️

STIR/SHAKEN Integration

Leverage STIR/SHAKEN cryptographic caller verification technology to verify spoofed numbers (supported in select regions, including North America).

📱

Dual SIM Card Recognition

Apply different blocking rules and actions based on which physical or eSIM card receives the incoming call or text message.

⚡

Flexible Blocking Actions

Choose your preferred blocking action: hang up immediately, mute the ringer, or answer-and-hang-up to prevent callers from leaving voicemails.

🔒

100% On-Device Local Privacy

All data is managed strictly on your device. The app includes zero online tracking databases and never uploads your contacts or call logs to external servers.

☁️

Multi-Cloud Backup & Restore

Seamlessly back up and restore your settings, rules, and plugins via WebDAV, Google Drive, and Microsoft OneDrive.

🧩 QuickJS Web Scraping Plugin Engine

One of the core strengths of YourCallYourRule is its powerful JavaScript plugin engine. Plugins can scrape online databases or query web APIs in real time to identify unknown caller IDs without relying on centralized servers.

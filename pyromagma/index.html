Q: How do PyroMagma's AI paper breakdowns and RAG Q&A work?

English: When opening a paper, PyroMagma builds a local vector index of the text. The LLM extracts key innovations and enables interactive RAG Q&A pinned directly to exact paper sections and mathematical equations.

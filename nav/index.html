VehereGo

Status bar island & Live Activity hub for Android. Automatically extracts parcel pickup codes (Cainiao, SF Express, Meituan) via OCR, manages PKPass vouchers, and tracks transit tickets in live capsules.

PyroMagma Academic

AI-driven academic literature reader. Browse paper card feeds (ArXiv, IEEE, Nature, PubMed), conduct interactive full-text RAG Q&A, and sync annotations seamlessly with Zotero and WebDAV.

PyroMagma Reader

The ultimate Legado (开源阅读) alternative for web novels, raw manga, and ebooks. Integrates local AI neural translation and live manga speech bubble OCR directly into the reader.

Legado Alternative Raw Manga OCR Local AI Translator EPUB/PDF Reader

Powerful, highly customizable call & SMS blocker for Android. Features blacklists, whitelists, regex filtering, STIR/SHAKEN caller verification, WebDAV cloud backup, and a web scraping plugin engine.
